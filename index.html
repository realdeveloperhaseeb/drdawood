Dr Dawood's speciality

Fillings & tooth repair

Operative dentistry is the art of repairing teeth: cavities, chips and worn or damaged surfaces. The aim is to save as much of your natural tooth as possible and make it look and feel right again.

  • Cavities cleaned and restored
  • Tooth-coloured fillings that blend in
  • Chipped or worn teeth rebuilt

Straighter smile

Braces

Crowded, gapped or crooked teeth are harder to clean and can affect how you bite. Braces move them gently into place over time, and they're not just for teenagers.

  • Consultation to look at your bite and what you want
  • Regular adjustment visits to keep things moving
  • Simple tips for cleaning around braces
Ask about braces
Nearly invisible

Clear aligners

A series of clear, removable trays that shift your teeth a little at a time. Most people notice them far less than metal braces, which is why they're popular with adults.

  • You eat and brush as normal, since they come out
  • A new tray every couple of weeks, with progress checks
  • Honest advice on whether aligners suit your case
Ask about aligners
For missing teeth

Dental implants

If you've lost a tooth, an implant replaces it from the root up: a small titanium post in the jaw with a crown on top. It's the closest thing to getting your own tooth back.

  • Assessment first, to check an implant suits you
  • Fixed and stable, it doesn't slip like a denture
  • Every step explained before we start
